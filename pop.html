Good to know

  • For a real flavour boost try adding paprika, sumac, black pepper or low sodium chicken stock powder to the popcorn once it is cooked.
  • If you don’t have a pot you could make this in a frying pan that has a lid. No lid and the popcorn will end up all over you kitchen!
  • Keep leftover popcorn in a container with a tight-fitting lid, or a zip lock bag. It will keep fresh for 1-2 days.
