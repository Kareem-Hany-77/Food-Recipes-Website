Equipment :

  • Spoon

  • Blender

Ingredients :

  • 1/2 cup nonfat dry milk powder

  • 3 cups chopped mango

  • 4 cups water

  • 2 cups ice cubes

Directions :

  1. Place milk powder, sweetened condensed milk, mango and water in

    blender; cover. Blend until smooth.


  2. Pour into pitcher with ice. Serve immediately.
